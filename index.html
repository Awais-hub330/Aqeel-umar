About Me

About Me

I'm Aqeel Umar, a graphic designer based in Lahore, Pakistan. My work includes social media carousels, advertising creatives, banners, flyers and logos. I enjoy visual storytelling, brand identity and creating clear layouts for digital and print communication.

My experience includes graphic design work at Grow Business Digital and design training at Qalam.IT under SeeBiz.com. I work with Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator, with basic knowledge of Figma, UI/UX and video editing in Canva.
